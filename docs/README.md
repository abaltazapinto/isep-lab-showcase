## Reading existing practical material

Practical-case documents have different levels of maturity. Their presence does not establish completion or validation. Read their sources and limitations before making project claims.

The [PL5 kernel-lab report](practical_cases/Desenvolvimento_Sistemas_Embebidos_lab/pl5_kernel_labs.md) distinguishes implementations, reported observations and measurement limits. Other material includes short learning/planning notes and overlapping gateway write-ups. These documents and their paths are retained unchanged in this foundation; case indexing and gateway consolidation remain future work.

The [root README](../README.md) still describes the existing showcase and its setup. Its broader framing will be updated separately. The documentation taxonomy is not yet fully implemented by the application's public project model.
